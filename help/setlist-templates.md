# 📋 Setlist Templates &amp; Service Plans

> Keep the shape of a service — welcome, songs, a reading, the sermon, a closing — and reuse it every week.

---

## 🎯 What this is

A service usually has a shape before it has songs. A **template** captures that shape as a list of labelled rows; applying one starts a brand-new setlist with those rows already there, ready for you to drop your songs into. This is separate from an ordinary setlist, which is just a plain list of songs.

---

## 📍 Applying a template

On the **Setlist** tab, next to **New Set List** is a **From Template** dropdown. Tap it, then tap a template's name — iHymns creates a new setlist with that template's rows already labelled. The dropdown is there for everyone, but which templates you see depends on who made them: your own, anything your organisation has shared, and anything made public.

---

## 🧩 Filling in the rows

Open the new setlist and you'll see a **Service plan** card. Each row shows its label (for example, "Opening" or "Praise 1") and, for a song row, a dropdown listing the songs already in that setlist — pick which one fills each slot. A row for something that isn't a song (a reading, a prayer, the sermon, and so on) just shows its label, with nothing to choose. A small counter tracks "3 of 6 songs chosen." Tap **Remove plan** if you'd rather drop the row structure and go back to a plain setlist.

---

## ✏️ Making your own template

On a setlist you've already built, open it and tap the **file-lines** icon in the button row — its tooltip reads **Save as template**. Give it a name and it's saved.

- Each song in the setlist becomes one labelled row in the new template (the row keeps the song's *title* as its label, not the song choice itself — so applying the template again gives you an empty slot to fill in, rather than repeating last week's song automatically).
- If the setlist you're saving already had a service plan (because it came from an earlier template), the new template keeps that same shape — including any non-song rows.
- You need to be signed in. A template you make this way is private to you until you or an administrator shares it more widely.

---

## 🛠 Managing your templates

From the **From Template** dropdown, tap **Manage templates…**. You'll see a numbered list of templates you created, and you can rename or delete one from there. You can only rename or delete templates you made yourself — a template someone has shared with you can be applied, but not changed or removed by you. Deleting a template doesn't touch any setlist already built from it.

Two exceptions worth knowing about:

- **Administrators can manage any template**, not only their own. That is what makes a shared template fixable when the person who wrote it is no longer around to fix it.
- **If the author's account is switched off or closed, their templates stay usable but become read-only.** You can still apply one to a new setlist exactly as before; nobody can rename or delete it except an administrator. Somebody else closing their account should never take your Sunday-morning service plan away with it.
