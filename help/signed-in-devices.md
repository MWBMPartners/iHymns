# 📱 Signed-in Devices

> See every device signed in to your account, and sign out one you don't recognise or no longer use.

---

## 🎯 What this is

Every phone, tablet or computer where you've signed in to iHymns stays signed in until you sign it out — including one you may have used once and forgotten about, like a borrowed laptop or a shared church computer. This screen lists them all and lets you end any of those sessions remotely.

---

## 📍 Where to find it

Sign in, then go to **Settings → Account &amp; Profile**. A **Signed-in devices** card lists each device currently signed in to your account.

---

## 📋 What you'll see

Each row shows a device's name if it has one (otherwise its platform, e.g. "iOS device"), and roughly how long ago it was last used (e.g. "3 days ago"). The device you're using right now carries a green **This device** badge and has no sign-out button of its own — to end your own current session, use the ordinary **Sign Out** button above, not this card.

Tap **Refresh** to reload the list.

---

## 🚪 Signing a device out

Tap **Sign out** next to any other device. You'll be asked to confirm — this is immediate and can't be undone from here: that device will need to sign in again the next time it's used.
