# 💡 Requesting a Song

> Can't find a hymn? Ask for it — and see what happened next.

---

## 🎯 What this is

If iHymns is missing a song you need, you can submit a request to our curators. If you're signed in when you send it, you can also come back later and see whether it's been reviewed, added, or declined.

---

## 📍 Submitting a request

Open **Request a Song** (linked from the bottom of any song page as "Report a missing song", or from the Help page). Fill in:

- **Song title** — required.
- **Songbook** — optional; helps a curator find it faster. As you type, matching songbook names from our catalogue appear below the box so you can pick the exact one — but you can still type any name, even one that isn't in our catalogue yet, since that may be exactly why you're asking us to add the song.
- **Extra details** — optional; a first line of lyrics or the writer's name goes a long way.
- **Your email** — optional, only if you'd like curators to follow up with you directly.

Tap **Send Request**. You'll get a confirmation and a reference number. If you're offline when you submit, iHymns saves it and sends it automatically once you're back online.

---

## 📋 Tracking your requests

Below the form, a **Your requests** section lists every request you've sent — but only if you were **signed in** at the time you submitted it. Each one shows:

- A status: **Pending**, **Reviewed**, **Added**, or **Declined**, with a plain-English line explaining what that means.
- A link straight to the song, once one has been added for it.

The list refreshes itself the moment a new submission succeeds, so a request you've just sent appears right away.

If you're signed out, you'll see a **Sign in** prompt here instead of a list — signing in is what lets iHymns connect a request back to you. A request sent while signed out can't be tracked, even if you sign in afterwards, because there was no account to link it to at the time.
