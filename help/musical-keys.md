# 🎹 Musical Key, Tempo &amp; Time Signature

> See what key a song is in, how fast it goes, and how that lines up with Transpose.

---

## 🎯 What this is

Where a curator has recorded it, a song page shows the song's original key, tempo (in BPM) and time signature — for example **Key: Bb · 96 BPM · 4/4**. It's a quick reference for a musician picking up an instrument, and it feeds straight into **Transpose**.

---

## 📍 Where to find it

Look just above the button row on any song page. If the badge doesn't appear, nobody has recorded a key for that song yet — it isn't hidden, there's simply nothing to show.

---

## 🎸 How it works with Transpose

Tap **Transpose** and use the **+ / −** buttons to shift the song up or down. The key shown updates to name the key you've transposed *into*, not just an offset — so if a song starts in G and you transpose up two semitones, the display switches to say A, not "+2".

---

## ✍️ Setting a song's key (curators)

If you curate songs, add the key from the **Song Editor**: open the song, go to the **Metadata** tab, and fill in the **Musical key** panel — a key picker (including both sharp and flat spellings, and major/minor), a tempo between 20 and 400 BPM, and a time signature. There's one **Save** button for all three; nothing saves until you press it.
