# 🎼 Set Lists

> Line up your service's songs, follow them hands-free while you lead, and share the running order with your team.

---

## 🎯 What this is

A **set list** is simply an ordered list of songs — the running order for a service, rehearsal, or event. Build one once, then use it to jump straight to each song in order instead of searching for the next one mid-service, share it with your team so everyone's looking at the same running order, and export or print it for anyone who needs a paper copy.

Your own set lists are saved on your device, so they're there even without a signal. A list someone has shared with you needs a connection the first time you open it.

If you also want to reuse the *shape* of a service — welcome, three songs, a reading, the sermon, a closing song — see [Setlist Templates & Service Plans](setlist-templates.md), which sits alongside everything below.

---

## ➕ Creating a set list and adding songs

1. Open the **Set Lists** tab and tap **New Set List**. Give it a name (e.g. "Sunday 14 September" or "Youth Night").
2. Open any song and tap its **Set List** button — pick which list to add it to, or create a new one on the spot.

There's no limit worth worrying about on how many set lists you keep, or how many songs go in one.

---

## ↕️ Putting the songs in order

Open a set list and you'll see every song it holds, numbered. Drag a song to a new position, or use the up/down arrow buttons next to it if dragging isn't convenient (for example, on a touchscreen where a plain tap is easier). The **×** button removes a song from the list — it only removes it from *this* list, the song itself is untouched.

---

## 🎚 Customising a song just for this occasion

Sometimes you want a song to run a little differently on one particular Sunday — skip a verse for time, repeat the chorus, or drop the bridge. Tap the **Arrange** (sliders) button next to a song in the set list to open a one-off arrangement editor: reorder or remove sections just for this appearance in this set list, with a live preview of the words as you go.

This only affects how the song plays back **from this set list** — the song's own master arrangement, and every other set list it appears in, are untouched. A small **"Custom Arr."** badge next to the song reminds you it's been adjusted; there's a button in the editor to clear it and go back to the song's normal order.

---

## ▶️ Following along during the service

Once your set list is built, tap **Use** (or simply tap any song in the list to jump straight to it) and a slim bar appears pinned to the bottom of the screen with **Prev** / **Next** buttons and a "3 of 6" position counter. Keep using Prev/Next and it carries you from song to song in the order you set — no need to go back to the list and pick manually each time.

The bar stays with you across pages until you tap its **✕** to leave set-list mode, so you can still browse elsewhere (search, favourites, and so on) without losing your place — coming back to a song puts the bar right back where you left it.

---

## 📅 Attaching a date

Open a set list and use the **Schedule** section to attach a date (and an optional note) — handy for keeping several upcoming services' set lists straight, or for looking back at what you sang on a given Sunday.

---

## 🔗 Sharing with your team

Tap **Share** on a set list to choose how others can use it:

- **Anyone with the link can view** — they can look through the songs and use Prev/Next to follow along. No account needed, and they can't change anything. You can choose whether your name shows on the page they see.
- **Anyone with the link can edit** — they can reorder and remove songs, again with no account needed unless you tick "People signed in to iHymns" to require one. Useful for handing the running order to a co-leader or the AV desk without them needing to log in.
- **Invite one specific person** — under **Collaborators**, invite someone by the email address of their existing iHymns account. This is the way to go if you want a named person to have lasting access rather than anyone who happens to have the link.

Every link you've created is listed under **Active links**, and you can revoke any of them at any time — the people who had it simply lose access from that point on.

---

## ⏳ Setting an expiry

Tap the **Expiry** button to give a set list an expiry date — useful for a link you only ever meant to be live for one particular service. Once that date passes, a shared link stops working and shows "no longer shared" instead of the songs; nothing is deleted, and you (the owner) can still open the set list normally, extend the date, or clear it back to "never expires."

## 📤 Exporting, printing & sharing a copy

- **Export as text** or **copy to clipboard** — a quick plain-text version of the running order (song titles, songbook and number), handy for pasting into an email or chat message.
- **Export as a ProPresenter playlist** — bundles the whole set list, in order, into one file ProPresenter can open directly as a service order, with every song's own words riding along inside — no separate import step needed.
- **Print** or **Save as PDF** — see [Exporting](exporting.md) for the full detail on printing, PDF, and CCLI copy reporting; a set list's PDF builds the whole thing as one document: a cover page, the running order, then every song's words in order.

---

## 🛠 If something goes wrong

- **A shared link says "no longer shared."** The set list's expiry date has passed, or the owner revoked that particular link. Ask the owner for a fresh one.
- **Prev/Next isn't following the order you expect.** Check whether a song has a custom arrangement (the "Custom Arr." badge) — that changes what plays back for that song, not the order of songs in the list itself.
- Still stuck? [Open an issue](https://github.com/MWBMPartners/iHymns/issues) with what you were trying to do and what happened instead.
