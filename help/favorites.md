# ⭐ Favorites

> Save and manage your favorite songs for quick access.

---

## Adding a Song to Favorites

1. Open any song's detail view
2. Tap the **star icon** (☆) next to the song title
3. The star fills in (★) to confirm the song has been saved

## Viewing Your Favorites

1. Tap the **Favorites** tab in the main navigation
2. Your saved songs are listed, sorted by most recently added
3. Tap any song to view its lyrics

## Removing a Song from Favorites

1. Open the song's detail view
2. Tap the **filled star icon** (★) to remove it
3. Alternatively, swipe left on a song in the Favorites list to remove it

---

## Notes

**If you are signed in, your favorites follow you.** They are saved to your account and appear on
every device you sign in on — your phone, a tablet, the church laptop. Add a favorite on one and it
turns up on the others.

**If you are not signed in, favorites stay on that device only.** They live in the browser's own
storage, so they are not shared anywhere and clearing your browsing data removes them. Signing in
afterwards keeps the favorites you already made and starts syncing them.

That is the whole of it: sign in if you want your favorites everywhere; you do not have to.
