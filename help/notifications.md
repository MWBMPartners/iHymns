# 🔔 Notifications

> Get a notification on this device when something you care about happens.

---

## 🎯 What this is

iHymns can send a notification straight to your phone, tablet or computer — for example, an announcement about a new songbook or a service update — without you having the app open. This is a per-device setting: turning it on here only affects the device you're using at the time.

⚠️ **This is a new feature and your site may not have switched it on yet.** If the card described below says notifications aren't available for this site, that's expected — nothing is broken, your site's administrator simply hasn't finished setting it up. Instructions here describe what happens once they have.

---

## 📍 Where to find it

Sign in, then go to **Settings → Account &amp; Profile**. If your site has notifications switched on, you'll see a **Notifications** card.

---

## 🔛 Turning it on

Tap **Turn on notifications**. Your browser will ask you to allow it — accept, and the card confirms "Notifications are on for this device." Tap **Turn off on this device** at any time to stop.

| What the card says | What it means |
|---|---|
| *"This browser does not support notifications. iOS needs the app added to your Home Screen first."* | On an iPhone or iPad, install iHymns to your Home Screen (Share ▾ "Add to Home Screen") before notifications can work at all. |
| *"Notifications are not switched on for this site yet."* | Your site's administrator hasn't finished setup. There's nothing to turn on yet. |
| *"You have blocked notifications for this site."* | Your browser remembers a previous "No" and won't ask again. Use the padlock or ⓘ icon in your browser's address bar to change it. |
| *"Notifications are on for this device."* | You're set — the button now lets you turn them off. |

---

## ✅ Choosing what you get

Once notifications are on, a short list of checkboxes appears — one per kind of message, for example **Announcements** (news, new songbooks and service updates) and **Test notifications** (a delivery test you or an administrator sends to your own devices). Untick anything you don't want; both are on by default.

---

## 🤔 If nothing arrives

This is a newly added feature. If you've turned notifications on but a notification you expected never shows up, it most likely means your site hasn't finished being configured to send them yet — there's no error to chase, and nothing more to try on your end.
