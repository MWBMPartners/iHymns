## ★ Lesson — squash-merge can silently drop commits

on the PR head, so the venues admin UI + external-systems hook were dropped (recovered via #1333). **Confirm a

is why the consolidated #1334 (carrying #1333's commits in history) merged cleanly — the already-on-alpha
content collapses out of a squash.
